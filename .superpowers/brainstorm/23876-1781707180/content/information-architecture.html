Information architecture for the redesign

Choose how Donote should organize file navigation, writing, outline, search, and settings after the full Element Plus migration.

A. Persistent three-pane workspace

Files, editor, and outline stay visible. Search and settings use Element Plus dialogs. Most familiar and stable, least transformative.

B. Right utility rail

Files remain on the left. Outline, search, and settings become right-side utility tools opened through icons and drawers. Cleaner editor by default.

C. Command rail and contextual drawer

A left rail controls workspace, search, outline, and settings drawers. More app-like and compact, but file navigation is one step less direct.
